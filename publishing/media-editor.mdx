The media editor is a built-in editing tool in the Composer that lets you touch up your video or image before you publish — without leaving Hivra or opening a separate app. You can crop and reframe for a platform's aspect ratio, adjust colors, add text overlays, trim or cut a video down to size, mute or boost the audio, and generate word-by-word captions, all from the same screen where you upload and caption your post.

<Frame>
  ![The editor panel with a video loaded showing caption bars in the timeline.](/images/the-editor-panel-with-a-video-loaded-sho-1f9dc3.webp)
</Frame>

<Info>
  Everything runs in your browser, on your own device. Nothing is uploaded until you click **Done** — at that point Hivra renders your edit and the result replaces the file you uploaded for that post.
</Info>

## Opening the editor

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload or select a file in the Composer">
    Drag a video or image into the Composer, or select a file you already uploaded from the thumbnail rail or media queue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hover the preview">
    Hover over the selected file's preview above the thumbnail rail. A pill labeled **Edit Video** (for video files) or **Edit Image** (for image files) appears pinned to the top of the frame.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Edit button">
    Click **Edit Video** or **Edit Image** to open the media editor. It opens as a full-screen tool with your media on the left and editing tools on the right.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  In a bulk upload session, the editor only ever affects the file that's currently active in the media queue — editing one clip's colors, crop, or text has no effect on the others. See [Per-file Composer context](/publishing/per-file-composer-context) for how each file keeps its own settings.
</Tip>

## The editing tools

The right-hand panel has tabs for each kind of edit. Images get **Crop**, **Adjust**, and **Text**. Videos get those three plus **Audio**.

### Crop, rotate, and flip

Under the **Crop** tab:

- **Aspect ratio** — choose a preset to reframe your media: **Original**, **Free** (drag to any shape), **9:16** (TikTok, Reels, Shorts), **1:1** (square feed), **4:5** (Instagram portrait), **16:9** (YouTube, X), or **1.91:1** (Instagram landscape). Drag the crop box on the preview to reposition it within the chosen shape.
- **Rotate & flip** — click **Left** or **Right** to rotate in 90 degree steps, or toggle the two **Flip** buttons to mirror the frame horizontally or vertically.
- **Straighten** (images only) — fine-tune the angle with a slider from -45 to +45 degrees. The image zooms in slightly so no empty corners show.

<Note>
  On the Free plan, the Crop tab shows a note that edits carry a small Hivra watermark, with a link to upgrade. See [Limits and plans](#limits-and-plans) below.
</Note>

### Color adjustments and filters

Under the **Adjust** tab:

- **Filters** — one-click presets: **Original**, **Vivid**, **Warm**, **Cool**, **Mono**, **Fade**, and **Drama**.
- **Adjust** — manual sliders for **Brightness**, **Contrast**, **Saturation**, and **Warmth**, each from -100 to 100. Double-click a slider to reset just that one value.

Filters and manual sliders stack — picking a filter and then nudging a slider adjusts on top of the filter.

### Adding text overlays

Under the **Text** tab, click **Add text** to drop a new text box onto your media (up to 10 per file, 280 characters each). Select any text in the list to edit it:

- **Content** — type your text, and use the **Emoji** button to insert an emoji at the cursor.
- **Font** — Sans, Serif, Mono, or Display.
- **Style** — Plain, Shadow, Outline, or Box (with its own box color).
- **Color** — pick a swatch or choose a custom color.
- **Size** and **Rotation** — sliders to scale and tilt the text.
- **Enter** / **Exit** (video only) — how the text animates in and out: None, Fade, Pop, Slide up, Slide in, Zoom, or Typewriter for entering; None, Fade, Slide down, Slide out, or Zoom for exiting.

Drag a text box directly on the preview to reposition it. On video, you can also drag its bar on the timeline to choose exactly when it appears and disappears. Click **Delete text** to remove the selected overlay.

### Trimming and cutting a video

Below the video preview, the timeline shows your clip as one or more connected parts:

- **Trim** — drag the gold handle at either edge of a part inward or outward to shorten or extend it.
- **Cut** — move the playhead to where you want to cut and click **Split** (or press the `S` key) to divide the clip into two parts there.
- **Remove a part** — select a part on the timeline and click **Delete** (or press `Delete` or `Backspace`) to drop it; the remaining parts play back to back.
- **Reset cuts** — click this link to restore the full, untrimmed clip.

<Tip>
  Press the spacebar to play or pause the preview, and use `Ctrl`+`Z` (or `Cmd`+`Z` on a Mac) to undo and redo any change you've made in the session.
</Tip>

### Auto-generated captions

Still under the **Text** tab, the **Auto captions** card turns your video's spoken audio into word-by-word captions:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a language (optional)">
    Leave **Language** set to **Detect automatically**, or pick a specific spoken language from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate captions">
    Hivra downloads a speech model to your browser the first time you use this (later runs reuse it), then transcribes the clip's audio on your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the detected language">
    If you left language on automatic, Hivra asks you to confirm the language it detected from the sound of the speech — accept the guess, or choose the correct one yourself. Your answer is remembered for next time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and adjust the captions">
    Captions appear on the timeline and over the video. Click a caption to edit its wording, drag it to retime it, or drag it on the preview to reposition where it sits on screen.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once captions exist, you can also set a **Font** (Clean or bold capitals), a **Highlight** color for the word currently being spoken, and sliders for **Size** and **Position** (Top, Middle, or Bottom). Use **Redo** to regenerate, or **Remove** to delete all captions.

<Note>
  Auto captions only work on a clip that has an audio track. Changing the language after captions already exist regenerates them in the new language.
</Note>

### Audio controls

Under the **Audio** tab (video only):

- **Remove sound** — mutes the clip entirely.
- **Volume** — a slider from 0% to 200% when sound isn't removed. Boosting past 100% is applied when you click **Done**; the live preview still plays at normal volume.

## Saving your edit

When you're happy with your changes, click **Done**. Hivra renders the edit on your device and shows a progress bar while it works — for video, this can take a little while depending on the clip's length and your device, so keep the tab open until it finishes. You can click **Cancel** during a render to stop it.

Once the render finishes, the edited file replaces the original upload for that post. If you close the editor without saving, click **Cancel** — if you made any changes, Hivra asks you to confirm before discarding them. **Reset all** clears every change made in the current editing session without closing the editor.

<Warning>
  Saving an edit replaces the file used for that post. The original upload is not published — only the edited version is sent when you publish or schedule.
</Warning>

## Limits and plans

| Limit | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Where it runs | Entirely in your browser, on your device — no part of the edit is processed on Hivra's servers |
| Browser support | Needs a recent Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox with video encoding support; **Edit Video** is disabled with an explanation if your browser can't support it |
| Max video length | Videos longer than 10 minutes can't be edited in the browser |
| Text overlays | Up to 10 per file, 280 characters each |
| Exported video | Up to 1080p, H.264 video with AAC audio |
| Exported image | Up to 4096px on the long side; PNGs stay PNG, everything else exports as JPEG |

<Warning>
  On the **Free** plan, every edited video or image carries a small Hivra watermark. Upgrading to **Creator** or **Business** removes it — see [Plans and pricing](/account/plans).
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The Edit Video button is disabled or missing">
    Your browser may not support the video encoding the editor relies on. Try a recent version of Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox. Editing images does not have this requirement.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Videos longer than 10 minutes can't be edited in the browser">
    The browser-based editor caps source videos at 10 minutes. Trim the file with another tool first, or publish it unedited.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The render seems stuck or very slow">
    Rendering happens on your own device and can take longer on an older computer or a long, high-resolution clip. Keep the tab open and in the foreground until it finishes — avoid switching to other heavy tabs while a render is in progress. If it truly stalls, click **Cancel** and try **Done** again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Auto captions detected the wrong language">
    Decline the detected language when Hivra asks, or open the **Language** dropdown afterward and pick the correct language — captions regenerate automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Generate captions is disabled">
    Auto captions need an audio track to transcribe. If your clip has no sound, the button stays disabled with a note explaining why.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My edit still has a watermark">
    Watermarking applies to every edit made on the **Free** plan. Upgrade to **Creator** or **Business** to remove it from new edits — see [Plans and pricing](/account/plans).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
