Unlike Hivra's other integrations, X charges per API call — reading a post, looking up a profile, publishing a tweet, replying, or liking a comment all cost money on X's side. To keep plans affordable, Hivra gives every paid subscription a monthly **X API budget** and tracks every X call your workspaces make against it. This article explains how the budget works, what happens as you get close to it, and what to do if publishing or syncing to X pauses.

<Info>
  X requires a Creator or Business plan to connect at all — see [Plans and pricing](/account/plans). The budget described here applies once you've connected an X account.
</Info>

## What the X budget is

The budget is per **subscription**, not per workspace — if your Business plan has 5 workspaces, they all draw from the same monthly amount.

| Plan | Monthly X budget |
| --- | --- |
| Creator | \$2.50 |
| Business | \$7.50 |
| Each add-on workspace | +\$1.00 |

<Note>
  X charges roughly \$0.005 for each post it returns, \$0.01 for each profile it returns, \$0.015 for each post, reply, or like you make, and \$0.20 for a post that contains a link. Hivra passes these costs through as a shared monthly budget instead of billing you per call.
</Note>

The budget resets at midnight UTC on the 1st of every month, regardless of your subscription's own billing date.

## Usage levels

Hivra tracks your spend against three thresholds. Each one changes what still works:

| Level | Reached at | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Within budget** | 0–79% | Everything works normally. |
| **80% used** | 80% | You get a one-time notice. Nothing is paused yet. |
| **Automatic updates paused** | 100% | Hivra stops refreshing X analytics, comment syncing, and follower counts on its own. Publishing, replies, and likes still work. |
| **Paused until next month** | 130% | Publishing to X, posting a first comment, replying, liking, unliking, deleting, and connecting a new X account are all paused until the budget resets. Your other platforms are not affected. |

<Warning>
  A call that would itself push your spend past the 130% ceiling is refused rather than allowed through — so actual spend never runs far past the limit.
</Warning>

## Checking your usage

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    Go to **Settings → Plan & billing**. The **X API usage** card appears for any Creator or Business subscription (it's hidden on the Free plan, since Free can't connect X).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the usage bar">
    The card shows how much of your monthly budget has been used, when it resets, and a status chip — **Within budget**, **80% used**, **Automatic updates paused**, or **Paused until next month**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the breakdown">
    Below the bar, a per-category breakdown shows what the spend went toward: Publishing, Replies/likes/deletes, Comment syncing, Analytics, Follower counts, Connecting X, and (where applicable) DM syncing and DMs sent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The info icon next to **X API usage** explains X's per-call pricing and how the budget is calculated, in case you want the specifics.
</Tip>

## What pauses at each level

### At 100% — automatic updates pause

Once your usage crosses the full monthly budget, Hivra stops making X calls it initiates on its own schedule:

- Refreshing analytics metrics (views, likes, impressions) for your X posts
- Refreshing comments in the [Engagement inbox](/engagement/overview) for X accounts
- Refreshing follower counts for connected X accounts

Nothing already fetched disappears — Hivra keeps showing the data it last synced. It just won't update again until the next reset, or until you reduce usage pressure by upgrading or adding a workspace.

### At 130% — publishing and actions pause

Once usage reaches 130% of the budget, anything that sends a request to X on your behalf is paused until the reset:

- Publishing a post to X from the Composer
- Posting a first comment to X
- Replying, liking, unliking, or deleting from the Engagement inbox
- Connecting a new X account

Your other connected platforms — YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and Threads — are never affected by the X budget.

## What you'll see in the Composer

If you try to publish while the X budget is at 130%, the Composer deselects X (or blocks you from selecting it) and shows an **X paused for this month** modal. It explains that the budget limit is reached, gives the date it resets, and confirms your other selected platforms are not affected. A **See X usage** link takes you straight to the usage card in Billing.

<Note>
  If you try to connect a new X account while the budget is at 130%, the authorization flow stops with a message explaining the X budget is reached and pointing you to **Settings → Billing**.
</Note>

## Notification preference

Hivra sends you a notice the first time your usage crosses 80%, 100%, and 130% in a given month, in-app and by email by default. To manage this:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Notification settings">
    Go to **Settings → Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find X usage">
    Under the **X usage** category, toggle **X API usage alerts** to control how you're notified when your monthly X budget reaches each threshold.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Getting a bigger budget

Since the budget is shared across your whole subscription, there are two ways to raise it:

- **Add a workspace.** Each add-on workspace adds \$1.00/month to your X budget, in addition to the extra workspace itself. See [Plans and pricing](/account/plans).
- **Upgrade to Business.** Business includes a \$7.50/month X budget, three times the Creator plan's \$2.50.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Why does only X have a usage budget, not the other platforms?">
    X is the only platform Hivra connects to that charges per API call. The other platforms (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, Threads) don't bill Hivra this way, so they have no equivalent budget.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My post to X failed with a budget-related error">
    This means your subscription's X budget reached 130% for the month. Check **Settings → Plan & billing** to confirm, then either wait for the reset on the 1st (UTC) or add a workspace / upgrade to Business for a bigger budget.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My X comments and analytics stopped updating, but I can still post">
    This is the 100% "automatic updates paused" level — background refreshes for X analytics, comments, and follower counts pause, but publishing and manual actions keep working until usage reaches 130%. The data shown is simply the last successful sync.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't connect a new X account">
    If the OAuth flow stops with a message about the X budget, your subscription has reached 130% usage for the month. Resolve it the same way as a paused post: wait for the reset, add a workspace, or upgrade to Business.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does deleting a draft or removing a platform affect my X usage?">
    No. Usage is only recorded for actual calls to X's API — publishing, replying, liking, and the background syncs described above. Drafting or editing a post in the Composer does not call X's API.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
