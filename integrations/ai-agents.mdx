Hivra's MCP (Model Context Protocol) server lets you run your Hivra workspace directly from AI apps like Claude, ChatGPT, and Cursor. Instead of clicking through the dashboard, you can ask your AI agent to draft and schedule posts, answer comments, or summarize your analytics.

Agents act exactly as you, respecting your team role and plan limits. For sensitive actions like publishing or deleting a post, the agent cannot proceed without you — it will ask you to confirm first.

## Connecting an AI app

Most AI apps connect to Hivra using a secure sign-in flow (OAuth), meaning you do not need to generate or copy any API keys.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find your app's setup steps">
    Go to **Dashboard → AI Agents**. Select your AI app (for example, **Claude** or **ChatGPT**) from the tab list to see its specific setup instructions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Hivra to your app">
    Follow the steps to add a custom connector or app. When prompted, paste the Hivra URL (`https://hivra.app/api/mcp`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and click Allow">
    The AI app will prompt you to authenticate. Follow the link, sign in to your Hivra account, and click **Allow** on the authorization screen. Your app is now connected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What agents can do

Once connected, your AI agent can manage almost every part of your daily workflow. Try asking it in plain words to:

- **Draft and publish posts** — write captions tailored to each platform, set visibility or disclosure options, and schedule or publish posts to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, and Threads.
- **Upload media** — an agent can generate a private upload link that works for 2 hours. You open the link (even on a phone), upload your videos or images, and tell the agent when you're done. Agents can also import media directly from public Google Drive, Dropbox, or OneDrive share links.
- **Manage engagement** — find unanswered comments, draft friendly replies, like comments, and read or send Instagram DMs. Agents can also set up and toggle DM automations.
- **Read analytics** — check views, reach, follower growth, and engagement for any period up to the last 28 days, or find the best time to post based on your recent performance.

## Confirming actions

To keep you in control, agents cannot independently execute destructive or irreversible actions. 

When you ask an agent to **publish**, **retry**, **approve**, or **delete** a post, or to **disconnect** a platform, the agent will pause and provide a confirmation link. You must click the link and confirm the action in your browser before the agent can complete the request. 

## Disconnecting an app

If you want to stop an AI app from accessing your Hivra account:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Agents">
    Go to **Dashboard → AI Agents**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Disconnect the app">
    Under **Connected apps**, find the app in the list and click **Disconnect**. It will lose access to your Hivra account immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Personal access tokens

If you are using the command line, custom scripts, or an MCP client that does not support OAuth sign-in, you can create a personal access token.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a token">
    Go to **Dashboard → AI Agents**. Scroll down to **Access tokens**, enter a name for the token (up to 60 characters), and click **Create token**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy your token">
    Copy the token immediately — this is the only time it is shown. Provide this token to your client as a Bearer token in the `Authorization` header.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You can have up to 10 active tokens at a time. If a token is compromised, click **Revoke** next to it in the list to disable it immediately.
</Note>

## Limits, plans, and permissions

- **Plan requirements** — the MCP server is available on every plan, including the Free plan. Features that require a paid plan in the dashboard (like comments, DMs, and analytics) require the same paid plan when used through an agent.
- **Usage quotas** — agent actions consume your plan's standard quotas, such as the 5 monthly uploads on the Free plan. 
- **Team roles** — an agent acts as you. If your role requires publishing approval, any post your agent schedules or publishes is submitted for approval instead of going live. Agents cannot manage team members or billing.
- **Rate limits** — each connected app or personal token can make 60 tool calls per minute and 5,000 tool calls per day.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My upload link expired">
    Upload links generated by an agent are valid for 2 hours. If a link expires before you finish adding your files, ask your agent for a new one.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The agent says I don't have permission">
    Agents respect your workspace role and plan. Ensure you are on a plan that supports the requested feature (like the Creator plan for analytics) and that the workspace owner has granted you the necessary permissions.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The app asks for an API key or header">
    Most MCP-compatible apps use OAuth sign-in and do not need a key. If your specific app requires an API key or header, create a personal access token in **Dashboard → AI Agents** and pass it as an `Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN` header.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
