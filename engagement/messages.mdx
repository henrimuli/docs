The **Messages** tab in **Engagement** brings your Instagram, Messenger, and X direct messages into a single inbox, so you can read and reply without opening each platform's app separately. It sits next to the **Comments** tab (for public comments) and the **Automations** tab (for auto-replies) — Messages is specifically for one-to-one conversations with the people who DM your connected accounts.

<Info>
  Messages supports **Instagram**, **Messenger** (Facebook Pages), and **X**. YouTube, TikTok, and Threads don't offer a way for apps like Hivra to send or receive direct messages, so those platforms never appear here.
</Info>

## Opening Messages

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Engagement">
    Click **Engagement** in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click the Messages tab">
    Select **Messages** from the tab bar at the top, next to **Comments** and **Automations**. The tab only appears once you have at least one Instagram, Facebook Page, or X account connected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a conversation">
    Conversations from every connected account appear in a single list, newest first, with unread conversations shown in bold and a count badge. Click one to open the thread.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  If you have no Instagram, Facebook Page, or X account connected, Messages shows a prompt to connect one instead of an empty inbox. See [Integrations](/integrations/overview).
</Info>

## Reading and replying to a conversation

Each conversation shows the sender's name (or @username, or avatar), which platform they messaged you on, and the message history. Messages sent by an automation are marked with a small **Automation** tag so you can tell them apart from messages you typed yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a conversation">
    Click any conversation in the list. On a phone or narrow window, the list and the open thread each take the full width — use the back arrow to return to the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your reply">
    Click the message field at the bottom of the thread and type your reply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Press **Enter** to send (use **Shift + Enter** for a line break), or click **Send**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your message appears in the thread immediately, labeled **Sending…**, instead of waiting for the platform to confirm it. Once Hivra hears back, the label disappears and the message looks like any other sent message. If it doesn't go through, the bubble is marked **Message failed to send** with a **Retry** link — click **Retry** to send the same text again without retyping it.

<Note>
  Leaving the conversation or closing Messages while a message shows **Sending…** does not cancel it — it keeps trying in the background and updates next time you open that conversation.
</Note>

Each platform caps how long a single message can be:

| Platform | Message length limit |
| --- | --- |
| Instagram | 1,000 bytes (roughly 1,000 characters of plain text) |
| Messenger | 2,000 characters |
| X | 10,000 characters |

## Filtering by platform

Above the conversation list, pill buttons let you filter to **All**, or to just **Instagram**, **Messenger**, or **X** — only the platforms you have connected and allowed appear as options.

## Reply windows: how long you have to respond

Instagram and Messenger only let you reply within a limited window after the other person's last message — Hivra never messages someone first.

| State | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Open** | Within 24 hours of their last message. A note under the message field counts down the hours left to reply. |
| **Extended (human agent)** | Between 24 hours and 7 days since their last message. Hivra sends the reply using Meta's "human agent" tag, which Meta must approve for Hivra's app before this works. A note shows how many days remain. |
| **Closed** | More than 7 days since their last message, or they haven't messaged you at all. The reply field is replaced with an explanation, and you can't send until they message you again. |

<Note>
  X has no reply-window restriction — you can reply to an X conversation at any time. Instead, every X DM you send (or receive) is billed against your workspace's monthly X API budget, as explained below.
</Note>

## Turning on X DMs

Because X charges for every DM its API delivers, X conversations are **off by default** per connected account. A card in the Messages tab invites you to turn them on, showing the cost: roughly \$0.01 for each DM received and \$0.015 for each one you send, drawn from your workspace's shared [monthly X API budget](/integrations/x-api-usage).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the cost notice">
    In the Messages tab, find the card for your X account prompting you to turn on DMs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Turn on X DMs">
    Hivra subscribes to that account's DM events. Messages sent before you turned it on are not shown — only new DMs from that point on appear.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If your workspace's monthly X API budget is used up, X DMs are automatically paused for every X account in that workspace until the budget resets. A card in Messages explains this and offers a **Turn back on** button once the budget renews. See [X API Usage](/integrations/x-api-usage) for the full budget breakdown.
</Warning>

## Keeping the inbox up to date

Messages is designed to open instantly and then quietly catch up:

- The tab first shows whatever Hivra already has stored, so it never waits on Instagram or Messenger to load.
- In the background, Hivra checks Instagram and Messenger for new conversations and messages at most every 10 minutes while you have the tab open.
- X conversations update as DMs arrive, since X delivers them directly once you've turned DMs on for that account.
- While the tab stays open and visible, the inbox also re-checks what's stored roughly every 30 seconds, so a reply you just sent or received shows up without a manual reload.

Click the refresh icon in the Messages header at any time to request an immediate sync instead of waiting for the next automatic one.

<Tip>
  If a background sync fails — for example, a platform API hiccup — Hivra keeps showing your last-loaded inbox rather than clearing it or showing an error. Use the refresh button to retry.
</Tip>

## Reconnecting an account for Messages access

If an Instagram or Facebook Page account was connected to Hivra before Hivra started requesting DM permissions, a banner appears at the top of Messages asking you to reconnect that account so it can send and receive DMs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Reconnect in the banner">
    This takes you to **Integrations**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reconnect the account">
    Reconnect the named account and accept the permissions requested. See [Connect Instagram](/integrations/instagram) or [Connect Facebook](/integrations/facebook) for the full connection flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until you reconnect, that account's conversations won't load or send in Messages.

## Plan requirement

Messages is part of **Engagement**, which requires a **Creator** plan or higher. If you're on the Free plan, you'll see an upgrade prompt instead of the inbox. See [Plans and pricing](/account/plans).

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I don't see a Messages tab in Engagement">
    The Messages tab only appears once you have at least one DM-capable account connected (Instagram, a Facebook Page, or X) and your plan includes Engagement. Connect an account from [Integrations](/integrations/overview) if you haven't already.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I can't send a reply — the message field is replaced with a note">
    This means the conversation's reply window has closed. For Instagram and Messenger, that happens more than 7 days after the other person's last message, or if they haven't messaged you yet — Hivra can't start a conversation on your behalf. Wait for them to message you (or message again) to reopen the window.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My X conversations aren't showing up">
    Check whether X DMs are turned on for that account in the Messages tab — they're off by default. If you already turned them on, a paused card means your workspace's monthly X API budget is used up; it resumes automatically once the budget resets, or you can upgrade or add a workspace for more budget. See [X API Usage](/integrations/x-api-usage).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A banner asks me to reconnect an Instagram or Facebook Page account">
    That account was connected before Hivra requested the permissions it needs to read and send DMs. Click **Reconnect** in the banner and complete the authorization again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A message has an 'Automation' tag I didn't write">
    That reply was sent automatically by a DM automation configured in the **Automations** tab — for example, a keyword-triggered flow — rather than typed manually from Messages.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My message shows 'Message failed to send'">
    Hivra couldn't deliver that message to the platform — for example, a closed reply window or a temporary API error. Click **Retry** under the message to send the same text again. If it keeps failing, confirm the conversation's reply window is still open and that the account's connection is healthy in [Integrations](/integrations/overview).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
